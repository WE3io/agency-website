# Brand v0

## Positioning
We help organisations ship clear, reliable digital work without noise. We are a small senior team that works in short, focused cycles and stays close to delivery.

## Promises we keep
- We show working progress early.
- We explain trade-offs in plain language.
- We keep scope and timelines explicit.
- We leave clients with maintainable outcomes.

## Promises we don’t make
- Overnight transformation.
- Unlimited scope for a fixed fee.
- Guaranteed outcomes we cannot control.
