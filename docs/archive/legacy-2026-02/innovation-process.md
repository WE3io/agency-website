# Innovation Process and Deliverables

## UNCOVER
- **Investigate**: Cultivate deep curiosity about the problem space; connect with users, business, partners, and third-party influences; ask probing questions to surface motives, constraints, opportunities, and unknowns; collect data and map the full context.
- **Provoke**: Reframe problems with first-principle thinking and unexpected angles; use analogies from other domains to broaden perspective; spark debate with “what would need to be true” prompts to surface assumptions and clarify what is known.

## EXPLORE
- **Play**: Suspend practicality to ideate widely; use whiteboarding, collaborative sessions, and creative exercises; favor a “yes, and…” culture to encourage serendipitous connections.
- **Prototype**: Use the fastest tool (paper, Figma, Miro, Gen AI, or code) to make ideas tangible; balance fidelity for clarity without over-polishing; give form to thinking via writing, sketches, wireframes, models, or storytelling to solicit actionable feedback.

## UNLOCK
- **Test**: Put prototypes in front of real users, partners, or stakeholders; gather quantitative and qualitative feedback; distill insights to refine, discard, or double down.
- **Action**: Plan implementation with business realities in mind (ROI, scale, operations); set KPIs; roll out to target users/markets; keep measuring and iterating.

## Types of Possible Deliverables
### UNCOVER (research, analysis, sensemaking)
- Market & Competitive Analysis
- Desk Research Reports
- Heuristic Evaluations
- Stakeholder Analysis
- User Research Reports (with quotes)
- Experience Maps
- Sitemaps Review (existing)
- Market Trends and Analysis
- Emerging Themes
- Documented Assumptions
- Concrete findings

### EXPLORE (ideation, conceptualization, prototyping)
- Problem Framing Documents
- Value Proposition Frameworks
- Opportunity Maps
- Persona Profiles
- Scenario Planning
- Blueprints & Models (e.g., service blueprints, business models)
- Prototypes
- Concept Storyboards
- Idea Backlogs
- Sitemaps (proposed)
- Interaction Design
- Mood Boards
- Design Principles
- Visual Design
- Concrete Findings (written strategy document)

### UNLOCK (implementation, testing, measurement)
- Roadmaps
- Usability Test Reports
- Service Design Blueprints
- KPI Frameworks
- Change Management Plans
- Go-to-Market Strategies

## Notes
This process is cyclical: uncover insights, explore possibilities, and unlock value with testing and action. Deliverables align to each phase and can be reused across cycles to push innovation forward.
