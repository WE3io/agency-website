# Business Context — WE3 Venture Studio & Consultancy

## Company Overview
**Company:** WE3  
**Model:** Senior AI-native venture studio and consultancy (studio + platform + community)  
**Crew Shape:** One senior engineer, one senior designer, one senior product operator, supported by AI and apprentices  
**Mission:** Help teams create the future by delivering clarity, prototypes, and validated paths faster than traditional, larger teams.  
**Focus Streams (2–3 yrs):** PE/corporate innovation, ambitious startups, WE3 internal ventures.

## Service Model
**Core Value Proposition:** Small, high-density senior crews deliver clarity and working concepts rapidly, using AI-native workflows and a repeatable clarity/delivery system.  
**Operating Systems:**  
- **Clarity System:** Structured elicitation, synthesis, and export to produce strong briefs.  
- **Delivery System:** Primary + secondary crews with apprentices; stable triads for each engagement.  
- **Talent Development:** Apprenticeships embedded in live projects to grow capacity and continuity.

## Offerings
- Problem/brief generation and alignment (AI-assisted clarity workflow)
- Rapid prototypes and validation loops (paper → Figma/Miro → code/AI builds)
- Product/service design, interaction design, and experience mapping
- Technical spikes and build-out to first usable versions
- Venture discovery from delivery insights; internal venture incubation
- Change and go-to-market support (roadmaps, KPIs, rollout planning)

## Target Clients
- PE firms and corporate innovation teams needing unblockers and clarity
- Startups and founders with big ideas needing a senior crew that can ship
- Investors needing fast diligence signals and credible build plans

## Approach & Principles (Crew Code)
- Do what’s right; build together; stay curious; lead with heart; take the risk; learn through play.  
- High autonomy, psychological safety, stable triads, and humor baked into delivery.  
- AI-native workflow across research, ideation, prototyping, and execution.

## Differentiators
- Senior-density triads outperform larger teams; faster time to first prototype.  
- AI-native clarity system reduces ambiguity before build.  
- Apprenticeship adds capacity without sacrificing quality; repeatable patterns feed ventures.  
- Culture of transparency, visible craft, and sharing in public to attract top talent.

## Success Measures (signals)
- Time to structured brief; time to first prototype  
- Validated brief accuracy; client satisfaction and repeat engagements  
- Delivery throughput and talent progression  
- Frequency of venture-worthy insights emerging from delivery work

## Current Risks & Mitigations
- **Risk:** Poor clarity inputs → **Mitigation:** Tight intake and brief generator.  
- **Risk:** Senior capacity constraints → **Mitigation:** Secondary crew coverage and clear staffing.  
- **Risk:** Apprentices slowing delivery → **Mitigation:** Supervision levels and QA playbooks.  
- **Risk:** Inconsistent delivery → **Mitigation:** Stable crews, rituals, and checklists.

## Strategic Priorities (near term)
1. Run the clarity workflow on real projects; refine the brief generator.  
2. Keep stable triads on each engagement; document patterns that generalize.  
3. Capture delivery insights into the venture pipeline; spin up 6-week experiments.  
4. Strengthen culture artifacts (playbooks, capability map, micro-site) to attract senior talent.
