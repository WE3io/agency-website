# Voice and tone

## Voice
- Calm, direct, and helpful.
- British English spelling and punctuation.
- Plain language, short sentences.

## Tone
- Confident without swagger.
- Friendly without being casual.
- Buyer-friendly, not studio-ish.

## What to avoid
- Hype, buzzwords, or grand claims.
- Vague promises (“world-class”, “best-in-class”, “cutting-edge”).
- Oversharing internal details.

## Evidence rules
- Every claim should be backed by a specific example, a measurable result, or a clear limit.
- If we cannot verify it publicly, don’t state it.
- Prefer “we do X” over “we can do anything”.

## Clarity rules
- Use concrete nouns and active verbs.
- One idea per sentence.
- If a reader could ask “what does that mean?”, rewrite it.
