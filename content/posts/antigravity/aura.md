# Aura

Evolving a brand for the next decade.

## The Challenge
A legacy brand struggling to stay relevant in a digital-first ecosystem.

## Our Approach
We treated the brand like a physical collection, curating new visual signatures that felt storied yet modern.

## The Outcome
300% increase in brand sentiment and a seamless rollout across all physical and digital touchpoints.
